Decision detail

500 W sprint response

Three separate 85-second blocks at 85 rpm: 100 W for 30 seconds, 500 W for 25 seconds, then 100 W for 30 seconds. These are outside the learning probes. The response table measures each 25-second effort and 30-second recovery separately, using full-rate recorded evidence. Sprint pass/fail decisions apply the recorded budgets to the entire 85-second block.

Rise time is elapsed time to 450 W. Settling requires 5 continuous seconds within ±20 W of the target. Missing measurements are shown as unavailable; a recorded null rise or settling time means the threshold was not reached or settled within that phase. Peak overshoot is measured above 500 W. Time outside ±20 W is measured during the 25-second sprint; recovery peak and time above 120 W cover the following 30 seconds.

Learning across the ride

“Fully learned” means measured support on the recorded tested grid, not the entire 300-cell firmware table. Partial and full starting fixtures come from production learning and are loaded into RAM; flash persistence is not tested.

Whole-ride scores include the pedaling pause; requested power is unattainable at zero cadence. Probe windows exclude that pause. These bands are provisional, not a firmware defect classification.

Reading these results
Absolute bands ask whether tracking meets provisional engineering budgets. Relative regression asks whether the candidate worsens versus the pinned baseline beyond a fixed allowance. Passing relative checks does not make a poor baseline acceptable. Both plant and delivered-sensor decisions matter.
Historical releases are rerun today under the same current simulator, model, adapter and scenarios. Release dates and experiment dates are different. Adapter-incompatible versions are marked unsupported and have no invented scores.
No BLE radio, ESP32 timing, scheduler contention or physical bench validation is claimed. Motor dynamics and sensor noise come from an approximate fitted model. Compare only runs with matching model, simulator, scenarios, seeds and adapter fingerprints.
Open evidence. Fixed budgets. No controller reimplementation.
